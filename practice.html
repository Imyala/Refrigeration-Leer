Practice

Five workshops, each one a different way of getting your hands on a machine. Pick the one that matches what you want to get better at — you can stop and switch at any time, and nothing here can be broken.

  1. 1 Cycle Simulator Get to know how a running system behaves A live vapour-compression system. Turn the compressor up, load the evaporator, break something on purpose — and watch every reading move together. It opens showing only the cycle; gauges and charts are one click away when you want them.

    10 min · start here if you are new

  2. 2 Service Bay Fit gauges to a machine without venting it Caps off, hoses on, crack the valve, purge, read, pack up as found. Do it out of order and the rig behaves the way a real one would — including losing charge to atmosphere.

    15 min · hands-on procedure

  3. 3 System Builder Learn where every component belongs Pipe the circuit yourself, in flow order. Drop each part into the run it belongs in and find out whether the system would actually run — and where a technician would have put the accessories.

    15 min · drag or click

  4. 4 Fault Diagnosis Workshop Work a fault out with no numbers handed to you A machine with something wrong with it. You decide where to put an instrument, read it, work out superheat and subcooling from the halves you have, then commit to a diagnosis.

    20 min · the real test

  5. 5 Technician Quiz Check yourself against a hidden fault The simulator, with the fault hidden. Read the instruments, name what is wrong, and find out whether you would have condemned the right part.

    10 min · scored

Prefer to be taught first? The course introduces each of these tools at the point you need it.
