Practice

Seven ways to get your hands on a machine. Nothing here can be broken.

  1. Cycle Simulator Get to know how a running system behaves A live vapour-compression system. Turn the compressor up, load the evaporator, break something on purpose — and watch every reading move together. It opens showing only the cycle; gauges and charts are one click away when you want them.

    10 min · start here if you are new

  2. Service Bay Fit gauges to a machine without venting it Caps off, hoses on, crack the valve, purge, read, pack up as found. Do it out of order and the rig behaves the way a real one would — including losing charge to atmosphere.

    15 min · hands-on procedure

  3. System Builder Learn where every component belongs Pipe the circuit yourself, in flow order. Drop each part into the run it belongs in and find out whether the system would actually run — and where a technician would have put the accessories.

    15 min · drag or click

  4. Fault Diagnosis Workshop Work a fault out with no numbers handed to you A machine with something wrong with it. You decide where to put an instrument, read it, work out superheat and subcooling from the halves you have, then commit to a diagnosis.

    20 min · the real test

  5. Control Circuit Workshop Find the electrical fault — safely A packaged unit that has stopped. A ladder diagram with test points, a meter that only reads where you put it, and a hidden fault. Ohms on a live circuit is refused; isolate, prove, test for dead, prove — then measure.

    20 min · scored on method too

  6. Procedure Trainers Pressure test, evacuate, recover and charge, braze The Code of Practice procedures as rigs you work on: the Code's own numbers in the rules, a clock you advance, a gauge that follows the ambient and the leak, and the order of work graded step by step.

    15 min each · four procedures

  7. Technician Quiz Check yourself against a hidden fault The simulator, with the fault hidden. Read the instruments, name what is wrong, and find out whether you would have condemned the right part.

    10 min · scored

Put it all together

Capstone job One plant, seven stages, one record Pipe a cool room, pressure test and evacuate it, charge and commission it, come back to a fault, and fix the electrics when it stops — each stage in the workshop that does that work, scored into one evidence record for your instructor.

2 hours · all the tools, in job order
