Fault Diagnosis Workshop

A machine with something wrong with it, and no numbers handed to you. Decide what to measure, fit the instrument, and read what it tells you.

How this works Decide, measure, then commit to a diagnosis

The method — the same one that works on site

  1. Decide, then measure. Every point on the machine is somewhere you could put an instrument. Click one to fit it — but ask first what the reading could change your mind about.
  2. Work the numbers. Superheat and subcooling each need two readings. Until you have both halves the number does not exist, and the panel says which half is missing.
  3. Commit. Name the fault — including the nerve to call a healthy machine healthy. Only then are you told what was really wrong.
