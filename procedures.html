Procedure trainers

The Code of Practice procedures, step by step, with the Code's numbers in the rules and the order of work graded

How this works Every button is an action on the rig; the order is the skill

Do the job the way the Code says, and watch what the shortcuts cost

  1. Read the job. The brief gives you the nameplate numbers — PS, cut-out setting, charge — and the Code references the rules come from.
  2. Take the actions. Choose the medium, set the pressure, pull the vacuum, weigh the charge. The rig answers each one the way a real one would: a wrong cylinder is refused, a repair under pressure is called out, a leak shows in the numbers.
  3. Advance the clock. Holds and pumps take time; the wait buttons move it, and the gauge follows the ambient, the leak, or the moisture.
  4. Read the order of work. Every step you were taught is listed; a step done out of turn says what that cost.
