Capstone job

One plant, seven stages, one record — every tool in the order a real job uses them

How this works Each stage opens the tool that does that work, and reports back here

A butcher's cool room, from empty pipework to the second call-back

  1. Take the stages in order. Pipe the circuit, pressure test it, evacuate, charge, commission and record the healthy readings, then come back to a fault, and later to a dead unit. Each one opens in its own workshop with the job's brief showing.
  2. Finish the job in the tool. When the workshop closes the job, the stage is recorded here with its score and you are pointed at the next one.
  3. The record is the point. The whole job goes into your evidence record, by unit of competency, and travels with your progress export to your instructor. A stage can be done again; the best score is kept.

    The tools grade method as well as answer, and so does this job: a stage passed with the order of work wrong scores lower than one done cleanly.
