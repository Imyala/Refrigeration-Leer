    Click a component

    Select any component in the diagram to learn what it does and how it changes the refrigerant. Or just watch the colours flow — each colour is a different refrigerant state.

    Gauge manifold

    gauge pressure

    Field gauges read pressure above atmospheric. The inner ring shows the saturation temperature at each pressure — the PT ring on a real gauge set. Needle vs. expected PT reading is how technicians judge a system.

    Pressure–Enthalpy (P–h) diagram

    R134a data

    The moving dot shows where the refrigerant is in its cycle right now. Inside the dome = a mix of liquid & vapour; left of it = liquid; right = vapour. Chart pressures are absolute.

    Performance

    from the cycle enthalpies

    COP = cooling delivered ÷ compressor work. Capacity is relative to this fluid's nominal point. Energy figures are per kg of refrigerant.

    Pressure–Temperature (PT) chart

    While liquid and vapour coexist (inside the coils), pressure and temperature are locked together — that is this curve. The dots mark where the system is evaporating and condensing right now.

    Target-pressure trainer

    gauge pressures

    Reading expected pressures straight off the PT relationship is how technicians judge whether a system is running correctly — practise until it's automatic.
