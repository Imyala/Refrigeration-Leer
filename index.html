Gauge manifold

gauge pressure

Field gauges read pressure above atmospheric. The inner ring shows the saturation temperature at each pressure — the PT ring on a real gauge set. Needle vs. expected PT reading is how technicians judge a system.

Pressure–Enthalpy (P–h) diagram

R134a data

The moving dot shows where the refrigerant is in its cycle right now. Inside the dome = a mix of liquid & vapour; left of it = liquid; right = vapour. Chart pressures are absolute.

Performance

from the cycle enthalpies

COP = cooling delivered ÷ compressor work. Capacity is relative to this fluid's nominal point. Energy figures are per kg of refrigerant.

Pressure–Temperature (PT) chart

While liquid and vapour coexist (inside the coils), pressure and temperature are locked together — this curve. Dots mark the current evaporating and condensing points. Below the dashed line the gauge reads vacuum.

gauge pressures
