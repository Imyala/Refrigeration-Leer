Refrigeration & air conditioning · training program

Learn refrigeration one stage at a time.

Six stages take you from what heat actually does, through reading gauges and diagnosing a fault, to working safely on real plant and to the Refrigerant Handling Code of Practice. Each stage is a short block of theory plus one hands-on tool — nothing new is opened until you need it. Behind the pathway sit three full technical streams for trade-course depth.

The pathway

    Going deeper: the specialist streams

    The pathway above is the guided program. Behind it sit three full technical streams — the trade-course depth an apprentice, a TAFE cohort or a university unit works through chapter by chapter. Open any of them from the course page.

    Already know your way around?

    The tools are introduced by the stages above, but they are open at any time.

    Cycle Simulator

    A live vapour-compression system you can drive, break and read. Starts on the simple view; more instruments appear as you need them.

    Service Bay

    Fit the gauges yourself: caps, hoses, purging and back-seating, with realistic consequences for getting the order wrong.

    System Builder

    Pipe a circuit yourself: drag the components into the loop in flow order and find out whether it would run — and where each accessory actually belongs.

    Fault Diagnosis Workshop

    A machine with something wrong and no numbers handed to you. Choose what to measure, fit the instrument, work out superheat and subcooling, then commit.

    Technician Quiz

    The fault is hidden. Read the instruments, work out what is wrong, and find out whether you would have condemned the right part.
