Refrigeration Learning Platform

For TAFEs, universities, RTOs and training departments

Try the simulator Start the course

Teach how refrigeration actually behaves — not just how it's drawn

Most refrigeration teaching material is static: diagrams, slides, videos. This platform is a live vapour-compression system students can probe, break and diagnose — wrapped in a structured 11-module course with quizzes, a final exam and a certificate. It runs in any browser, imports into your LMS as a SCORM package with gradebook reporting, and needs no installation, no server and no student accounts.

See the Technician Quiz — the flagship exercise Open a sample lesson

Interactive simulator

An animated cycle with real per-refrigerant saturation data: colour-coded state flow, a live P–h diagram, an analog gauge manifold with PT rings, compressor-speed and load sliders, and kPa/bar/psi + °C/°F units.

Fault diagnosis that behaves like the field

Ten faults with realistic gauge signatures — from low charge to leaking compressor valves. Technician Quiz mode hides the fault and makes students read gauges, superheat and subcooling to find it, with scored feedback.

Hands-on Service Bay

Students fit the gauges themselves: remove caps, connect and purge hoses, crack valves off the back seat, take readings and pack up as found — with realistic consequences for venting, skipped purges and front-seating a running discharge valve.

Structured 11-module course

39 lessons from heat fundamentals through components, refrigerants, electrical, diagnosis, repair procedures and safety — closing with a full module on the Australia and New Zealand Refrigerant Handling Code of Practice (2025 edition), taught clause by clause and cited from the lessons it governs. Every lesson ends in a quiz with explanations; lessons deep-link the simulator into the exact scenario being taught.

Exam & certificate

A final exam drawn randomly from the whole question bank — two questions per module, so it grows with the course — with a fresh paper every attempt, an 80% pass mark, and a printable certificate of completion with a verifiable ID.

Instructor tools

A cohort dashboard that aggregates student progress exports — per-module completion, exam results, CSV download — entirely in the instructor's browser. No student data ever leaves your institution.

LMS-ready (SCORM 1.2)

Import into Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard or D2L as a SCORM activity. Completion and exam scores report to the gradebook, and each learner's progress is stored against their LMS account.

Run a pilot

The fastest way to evaluate the platform is a one-term pilot with a single class: import the SCORM package (or just share the link), assign two modules and the Technician Quiz, and compare against your current materials. The pilot playbook in docs/SALES_OVERVIEW.md has the checklist, success metrics and a feedback template.
