# Source Coverage

Which source document each course module was written from, and how much
teaching material came out of it. This is the audit trail behind the claim
that the syllabus covers the source library completely.

**Totals: 54 modules, 437 lessons, 1778 quiz questions, roughly 560,000 words of
teaching content.**

## How the content was produced

1. Every page of every source PDF was rendered and OCR'd — **1,914 pages** across
   the AIRAH volumes, the electrical text and both Parts of the Refrigerant
   Handling Code of Practice.
2. Each chapter's text was read in full, and its topics, procedures, data tables
   and worked examples were mapped to a module of lessons.
3. Lessons were then **written from scratch**. Terminology, definitions, standard
   values, procedures, formulae and numerical data are carried across unchanged —
   those are facts and must stay correct — but every sentence of prose is new.
4. An automated originality check then compared every lesson against the OCR of
   the sources, looking for any run of matching words. Passages sharing 16 or more
   consecutive words were rewritten. After that pass, the longest shared run of
   prose anywhere in the course is **15 words**, and those are unavoidable strings
   of standard technical phrasing.

## Known gaps in the source scans

The supplied PDFs are photocopier scans, and a few book pages are missing from
them — the page numbering inside the scan skips. Where a gap fell inside a topic,
the lesson was completed from standard Australian trade practice rather than left
short, and no figures or catalogue values were invented. The affected areas are:

- **Vol 1 Ch 3 (Condensers)** — part of the tube-in-tube, shell-and-tube and
  evaporative-condenser construction pages.
- **Vol 1 Ch 6 (Ancillary equipment)** — parts of EPR/hot-gas-bypass, water
  regulating valves, head-pressure valves, sight glasses, heat exchangers, oil
  separators, mufflers and receivers.
- **Vol 1 Ch 7** — the parallel-compressor / multiple-system capacity-control
  introduction.
- **Vol 1 Ch 4** — some contact-cooling and liquid-overfeed detail.

If you can supply clean scans of those pages, the affected lessons can be
re-checked against the originals.

## Duplicate source files

`Electrical Chpt 9.pdf` and `Electrical Chpt 9t.pdf` are two scans of the same
chapter (single-phase a.c.); the more complete scan was used and the other was
consulted only to resolve garbled passages. `Electrical Merged.pdf`,
`Refrigeration Airah 1.pdf` and `Refrigeration Airah 2.pdf` are whole-book
merges of the per-chapter files and were not processed twice.
