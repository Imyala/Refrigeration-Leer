System Builder

Pipe the circuit yourself — every component in flow order, checked the way a teacher would check it

Follow the refrigerant — that is the whole method

  1. Start at the compressor. It is fixed at the top of the loop, because that is where a circuit is always read from. Everything else you add follows the flow round to it again.
  2. Drag a part in, or click it. Drop it between two components to place it there; use the ▲ ▼ buttons on a placed part to move it, and × to take it out. Every action works from the keyboard.
  3. Read what the analysis tells you. An error means the system would not run. A note means it would run, but a technician would have piped it differently — and it says why.
