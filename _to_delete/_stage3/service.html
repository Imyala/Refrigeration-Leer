Service Bay

Fit the gauges yourself: caps, hoses, purging, back-seating — with real consequences

The sequence — in this order, every time

  1. Choose and prove. Pick hoses rated for the machine in front of you, in good condition and with low-loss ends — then prove the gauges read zero before they go anywhere near the system.
  2. Expose and connect. Remove the right caps first, then connect hoses only to ports that are actually available.
  3. Purge and read. Crack the valve off the back seat, purge only with pressure behind the hose, and watch the gauges respond.
  4. Pack up as found. Back-seat the valves, refit the caps, and leave no venting, loose hose or unfinished job behind.

The machine

A stem-type service valve: back-seated (fully anticlockwise) isolates its gauge port for normal running · cracked a turn off the back seat feeds system pressure to the port · front-seated (fully clockwise) closes the line itself. The procedure: caps off → hoses on → crack → purge → read → pack up as found. Purge briefly and only with pressure behind the hose — the Refrigerant Handling Code of Practice expects emissions kept to a practical minimum.
