Procedure training

Practise the service sequence in the right order, with the same consequences a learner would expect in the field.

The Service Bay now reads more like a guided station: uncover the ports, connect the hoses, purge with pressure behind them, take your readings, and leave the machine exactly as you found it.

1

Expose and connect

Remove the right caps first, then connect hoses only to ports that are actually available.

2

Purge and read safely

Crack the valve off the back seat, purge only when there is pressure behind the hose, and watch the gauges respond.

3

Pack up as found

Back-seat the valves, refit the caps, and check that you leave no venting, loose hose, or unfinished job behind.

A stem-type service valve: back-seated (fully anticlockwise) isolates its gauge port for normal running · cracked a turn off the back seat feeds system pressure to the port · front-seated (fully clockwise) closes the line itself. The procedure: caps off → hoses on → crack → purge → read → pack up as found. Purge briefly and only with pressure behind the hose — the Refrigerant Handling Code of Practice expects emissions kept to a practical minimum.
