# References & Standards Alignment

The course is aligned to Australian regulatory requirements, standards and
the standard Australian trade texts. Every lesson carries a **References &
alignment** panel citing its sources at topic level.

## How alignment works (and what it is not)

- **Publicly available regulatory material** — the Refrigerant Handling Code
  of Practice, ARC licensing information, and legislation — is **paraphrased
  with citation** where lessons state requirements (recovery obligations,
  licence classes, evacuation methods, leak-test rules).
- **Paid, copyrighted publications** — AS/NZS standards and the ARAC
  textbooks — are **cited at topic level only**. The course does not
  reproduce their text, tables or figures. Learners and institutions must
  work from current editions, which RTOs are required to hold.
- Codes and standards **change**. Where a specific number is safety- or
  compliance-critical (test pressures, vacuum depths, charge limits), lessons
  teach the method and the widely used industry values, and direct the
  learner to the current edition of the governing document as the authority.
- No endorsement is implied: ARCtick is the Australian Refrigeration
  Council's scheme; AIRAH, Standards Australia and the ARC have not reviewed
  or endorsed this course.

## Maintenance

When a new edition of the Code of Practice or a cited standard is published:
search the course content (`js/course1.js`, `js/course2.js`) for the
reference strings above, review the affected lessons, and update both the
lesson text and this file. The test suite enforces that every lesson carries
at least one reference.
